Not yet proven

A completed capture of real funds on the production account. No real card has been charged. Test cards only function inside a processor's sandbox; they are rejected in live mode by design, and repeatedly submitting them against a live gateway risks the merchant account being flagged.

Closing this requires one real-card transaction on the live checkout. Airwallex's own go-live checklist prescribes exactly this: "Use a real card and input a nominal amount." The lowest amount eTeacher's CRM will issue is $129.00 (trial enrolment); the standard amount is $279.80. The amount is set server-side by eTeacher and cannot be overridden from our side — four override methods were attempted and all were ignored.
